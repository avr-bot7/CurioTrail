Why this is different

A tradition existing doesn’t mean you’re invited

Most travel sites stop at “go here”. We don’t list a workshop or a visit until the person who does the work says yes. Until then, you can still read about it — properly.

05 · How we work

Four rules we don’t bend

01

Interest first

You start from what you enjoy, not a map. Every match says why it fits.

02

Show the work

Process, materials, history and who carries it on — with sources and a review date.

03

No invented visits

Watching or joining is shown only after the practitioner confirms it.

04

Credit the community

Knowledge is attributed. Practitioners can correct anything written about them.
